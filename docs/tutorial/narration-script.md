# Skuuul-Line Pro guided tour — narration script

Video: `Skuuul-Line-Pro-Tutorial.mp4` · length 13:18 · 89 lines · narrated (British English voice) with background music

Read each line when its time comes up. The same words appear as captions in the video, so the voice should match them closely; small natural changes are fine. A warm, friendly, unhurried tone works best — like a head teacher showing a visitor around the school.

## Welcome  `0:00`

- **0:01** Akwaaba! Welcome to the Skuuul-Line Pro guided tour.

## 1. Signing in  `0:07`

- **0:09** Akwaaba! Welcome to Skuuul-Line Pro, the complete management system for Bright Future International School — from Creche all the way to Diploma.
- **0:19** Everyone signs in to their own portal: administrators, teachers, parents, students, the accountant, librarian, storekeeper, transport officer, canteen manager and support staff.
- **0:33** In demo mode you can open any portal with one click. Each portal shows only what that person is allowed to see.
- **0:42** A school can switch on secure sign-in, with a password and a verification code sent to the phone.
- **0:49** Staff use their email or phone, students use their admission number, and parents can create their own account.
- **0:57** Let's go back and open the Administrator portal.

## 2. Admin dashboard  `1:03`

- **1:05** This is the head's dashboard. At a glance: enrolment, staff, fees collected this term, and fees still outstanding.
- **1:15** The Needs attention panel flags what matters today — gate arrivals, low stock, overdue library books and pending leave.
- **1:24** Charts show income against expenditure, enrolment by section and the attendance trend.
- **1:31** The menu on the left groups every module. On a phone it becomes a bottom bar.
- **1:38** Search finds any student, staff member or page instantly. Press Control K from anywhere.
- **1:47** And there is a dark mode for evening work.

## 3. Admissions  `1:53`

- **1:57** Admissions tracks every applicant from application, to entrance exam, interview, offer and enrolment.
- **2:05** For an applicant who has been offered a place, you can print a formal admission letter with the fees to pay.
- **2:12** One click on Enrol creates the student, the parent record and the first fee bill together.
- **2:18** And here is the new student, ready in the system.

## 4. Students & photos  `2:24`

- **2:27** The Students page lists every learner. Filter by section, class or status, or export to Excel.
- **2:36** Let's open Kofi Mensah in JHS 2.
- **2:41** His profile brings everything together: personal details, parent contacts, medical notes, results, attendance, fees, library and conduct.
- **2:53** To add a photo, tap the camera button. You can upload a picture or take one with the camera.
- **3:00** Drag and zoom to frame the face, then save.
- **3:05** The photo now appears on his ID card, report card, gate badge and everywhere he is listed.
- **3:13** The tabs show his academic results, attendance record and fee statement.

## 5. Staff, HR & payroll  `3:21`

- **3:26** Staff and HR keeps every teacher and worker, with roles, qualifications, leave requests and approvals.
- **3:35** Each staff member has a profile with their teaching load, pay and a printable staff ID card.
- **3:44** Payroll calculates salaries every month with Ghana SSNIT and PAYE deductions, ready for the bank.
- **3:51** Click any line to print a payslip.

## 6. Classes & timetable  `3:56`

- **4:00** Classes are organised by section: Early Years, Primary, JHS, SHS programmes and Diploma.
- **4:09** Open a class to see its subjects, the teacher for each one, and the students.
- **4:16** The timetable shows the whole week for any class, with breaks and lunch. The administrator can edit it here.

## 7. Attendance register  `4:25`

- **4:28** Teachers take the daily register in seconds. Mark everyone present with one tap…
- **4:36** …then change anyone who is late or absent.
- **4:40** Save the register, and with one more tap, parents of absent children receive a text message.

## 8. Gradebook & report cards  `4:48`

- **4:51** In the gradebook, teachers enter class scores out of thirty and exam scores out of seventy.
- **4:58** Totals, grades and positions are calculated automatically, using the GES, WASSCE or Diploma grading scale.
- **5:09** Report cards are generated for the whole class, ranked, with remarks and the BECE aggregate for JHS.
- **5:18** Open one to print it, or print the whole class at once.

## 9. Learning (LMS)  `5:24`

- **5:27** The learning platform lets teachers post lesson notes, slides, worksheets and video links for each class.
- **5:35** Assignments show who has submitted and what still needs marking.
- **5:41** And quizzes mark themselves the moment a student submits.

## 10. Fees & finance  `5:47`

- **5:49** This is the accountant's portal: money billed and collected this term, arrears and expenses.
- **5:57** Fees and Finance holds every invoice, payment, arrears list, fee structure and expense.
- **6:05** Recording a payment takes seconds — Mobile Money, cash, bank transfer or cheque.
- **6:13** Save it, and an official receipt is ready to print or share.
- **6:18** The Arrears tab lists everyone who owes, with one-tap SMS reminders to parents.

## 11. Canteen  `6:27`

- **6:30** The canteen manager sells food from a simple point of sale — jollof, waakye, banku and tilapia, kelewele, sobolo and more.
- **6:40** Tap items to add them to the order…
- **6:47** …choose the customer and payment — cash, MoMo, or add it to the student's fee bill — and complete the sale.
- **6:55** Plan the weekly menu, track kitchen stock, and watch daily income and best sellers.

## 12. Store, library, transport & more  `7:03`

- **7:06** The store tracks uniforms, books and supplies, with low-stock alerts and purchase orders.
- **7:14** The library manages the catalogue, loans and returns, and works out overdue fines automatically.
- **7:23** Transport manages buses, routes, drivers and riders, trips and maintenance.
- **7:31** For boarding schools: halls, bed allocation and exeats.
- **7:38** And health and discipline records keep every infirmary visit and incident on file.

## 13. QR gate attendance  `7:45`

- **7:49** Gate Attendance shows who has arrived this morning, who is late, and who has not come in yet — for students and staff.
- **7:57** At the entrance, a tablet runs the gate kiosk.
- **8:02** It shows a QR code that changes every thirty seconds. Students and staff scan it with their phone to clock in and out.
- **8:11** Pupils without phones hold up their ID badge to the kiosk camera. Their photo appears so the security officer can confirm it is really them — and the parent gets a text.

## 14. Teacher portal & clock-in  `8:29`

- **8:31** This is Mr. Kwame Asante's portal: his lessons for today, work to mark, and a reminder to take the register.
- **8:39** He clocks in by scanning the gate code — or typing the six-digit code shown on the kiosk.
- **8:46** His arrival time is recorded, and late arrivals are flagged automatically.

## 15. Live classes  `8:53`

- **8:55** Teachers can teach live, with camera, microphone, screen sharing and chat. Each class has its own code and invite link.
- **9:05** Before starting, check the camera and microphone, and choose low-data or audio-only mode for slow connections.
- **9:17** Students join with the class code or invite link. Here, Kofi has just joined from his phone.
- **9:25** Students can raise a hand, chat, and turn their camera or microphone off at any time.
- **9:32** The teacher can mute everyone, lock microphones or cameras, remove a student, and end the class for all. Attendance is saved automatically.

## 16. Parent portal  `9:48`

- **9:50** Mrs. Akosua Mensah has two children in the school. Her portal shows each child's attendance, average and fees owing.
- **10:00** She can see their results for every term and download the report card.
- **10:07** And she can pay school fees with Mobile Money, right from her phone.
- **10:22** She can also check the school bus route and stops, and the canteen menu.

## 17. Student portal  `10:28`

- **10:30** Kofi's own portal shows today's lessons, homework due, quizzes and his results.
- **10:38** He can take a quiz on his phone, with a timer, and see his score straight away.
- **11:01** He can browse the library catalogue and check his borrowed books.

## 18. Staff portals  `11:07`

- **11:09** Every staff member gets a portal built for their job. The librarian sees overdue books and fines.
- **11:18** The storekeeper sees stock value and the reorder list.
- **11:23** The transport officer sees every route, bus and rider.
- **11:29** And support staff — drivers, security, cleaners and cooks — can clock in and read school notices.

## 19. Super Admin settings  `11:37`

- **11:41** The Super Admin sets up the system for each school: name, logo and colours.
- **11:47** Choose a colour theme, and the whole app changes instantly.
- **11:53** Modules a school does not use — like hostel or canteen — can be switched off.
- **11:59** Login and security controls password rules, verification codes, parent sign-up and account lockout.
- **12:07** And the Users tab approves new parent accounts and resets passwords.

## 20. Works offline  `12:15`

- **12:18** Skuuul-Line Pro keeps working even when the internet goes off.
- **12:24** The orange bar shows we are offline — but every page still opens, and all your work is saved on the device.
- **12:33** Text messages sent while offline wait in a queue…
- **12:39** …and go out automatically as soon as the connection returns.

## 21. On your phone  `12:47`

- **12:49** On a phone, Skuuul-Line Pro looks and feels like an app, with a bottom menu.
- **12:55** Install it: on iPhone, Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen. On Android, Chrome menu, Install app.
- **13:05** Then it opens straight from the home screen — even without data.

## Thank you  `13:10`

- **13:11** Medaase for watching. Sign in, pick a portal, and explore.
